# Ryzen Store Shopping Cart

An interactive console shopping cart program where users can buy gaming titles, configure quantities, and view a fully breakdown invoice detailing discounts and GST.

---

## Features

* **Game Catalog**: Choose from 10 popular games including Helldivers 2, Cyberpunk 2077, and GTA VI.
* **Automatic Discounts**:
  * **2.5% Off**: For total orders between ₹5,000 and ₹9,000.
  * **12% Off**: For total orders above ₹9,000.
* **Tax Calculation**: Automatically calculates 18% GST (split equally into 9% SGST and 9% CGST).
* **Detailed Receipt**: Shows the initial cost, applied discount, taxable amount, tax breakdown, and the total final bill.
